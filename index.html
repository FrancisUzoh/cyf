Dogs are wonderful pets because they are loyal, friendly and loving. They can provide you companionship, protect your homes, and bring happiness to families.

Alasca Malamute

The Alaskan Malamute is a strong, friendly and hardworking dog breed. It was originally developed for pulling heavy sleds across snowy Arctic landscapes.

Cane Carso

The Cane Corso is a powerful, intelligent and loyal Italian dog bree. it is traditionally used for guarding property, protecting families and working.

German Shepherd

The German Shepherd is an intelligent, loyal and courageous dog breed, widely known for its working abilities, protective nature and excellent trainability.
